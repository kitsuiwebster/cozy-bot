💎 CozyPoints

How to earn points?

Base Points

Active Listening

  • +1 point per minute listening in voice with the bot
  • +5 points for joining a session (connection bonus)

Loyalty Bonuses

Continuous Listening to the Same Sound

  • +50 points after 30 minutes on the same sound
  • +100 points after 1 hour on the same sound
  • +500 points after 12 hours on the same sound

Streak Bonuses

Daily Activity

  • +[streak days] points every 10 minutes in voice
  • Example: 7-day streak = +7 points every 10min of listening

Exploration Bonuses

Complete Categories

+50 points for listening to all sounds in a category:

  • 🌧️ Rain
  • 🌊 Sea
  • ✨ Sparkles
  • 🎶 Music

Bonus granted only once per category

Level System

Level Bonus

  • Level 2: +20 points
  • Level 3: +30 points
  • Level 4: +40 points
  • Level 5: +50 points
  • Level 6: +60 points
  • Level 10: +100 points

When you reach level X, you get +X×10 bonus points

Achievement System

Achievement Bonus

Each achievement gives +100 points automatically

Level Achievements

  • 🥉 Bronze (level 5)
  • 🥈 Silver (level 10)
  • 🥇 Gold (level 15)
  • 💎 Diamond (level 25)
  • 👑 Master (level 50)

Time Achievements

  • ⏰ First Hour (1h listening)
  • 🕐 Dedicated Listener (10h)
  • ⏰ Time Master (50h)
  • 🕰️ Eternal Listener (100h)
  • ♾️ Time Lord (1000h)

Streak Achievements

  • 🔥 Week Warrior (7 days)
  • 📅 Monthly Master (30 days)
  • 💯 Streak Legend (100 days)
  • 🗓️ Year Champion (365 days)

Sound Achievements (Legacy)

  • 🌧️ Rain Master (50+ rain sessions)
  • 🌊 Sea Master (50+ sea sessions)
  • ✨ Sparkles Master (50+ sparkles sessions)
  • 🎵 Music Master (50+ music sessions)
