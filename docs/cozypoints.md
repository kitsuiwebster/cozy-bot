# CozyPoints

## Base Points

### Active Listening
- **+1 point per minute** listening in voice with the bot
- **+5 points** for joining a session (connection bonus)

## Loyalty Bonuses 

### Continuous Listening to the Same Sound
- **+50 points** after 30 minutes on the same sound
- **+100 points** after 1 hour on the same sound  
- **+500 points** after 12 hours on the same sound

## Streak Bonuses

### Daily Activity
- **+[streak days] points every 10 minutes** in voice
- Example: 7-day streak = +7 points every 10min of listening

## Exploration Bonuses

### Complete Categories
- **+50 points** for listening to all sounds in a category:
  - 🌧️ Rain
  - 🌊 Sea
  - ✨ Sparkles
  - 🎶 Music
- Bonus granted **only once** per category

## Level System

### Level Requirements (Quadratic Progression)
- **Level 1→2**: 50 points needed (1²×50)
- **Level 2→3**: 200 points needed (2²×50)  
- **Level 3→4**: 450 points needed (3²×50)
- **Level 4→5**: 800 points needed (4²×50)
- **Level 5→6**: 1250 points needed (5²×50)

### Level Bonus
- **Level 2**: +40 points
- **Level 3**: +60 points  
- **Level 4**: +80 points
- **Level 5**: +100 points
- **Level 6**: +120 points
- **Level 10**: +200 points

- When you reach level X, you get +X×20 bonus points

## Achievement System

### Achievement Bonus
- **Each achievement gives +100 points automatically**

### Level Achievements
- 🥉 **Bronze** (level 5)
- 🥈 **Silver** (level 10)
- 🥇 **Gold** (level 15)
- 💎 **Diamond** (level 25)
- 👑 **Master** (level 50)

### Time Achievements
- ⏰ **First Hour** (1h listening)
- 🕐 **Dedicated Listener** (10h)
- ⏰ **Time Master** (50h)
- 🕰️ **Eternal Listener** (100h)
- ♾️ **Time Lord** (1000h)

### Streak Achievements
- 🔥 **Week Warrior** (7 days)
- 📅 **Monthly Master** (30 days)
- 💯 **Streak Legend** (100 days)
